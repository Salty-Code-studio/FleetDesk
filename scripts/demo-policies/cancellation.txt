CANCELLATION AND CHANGES
Yabi Demo Rentals. Demo text, not legal advice.

1. Free cancellation up to 48 hours before pickup. You get everything back.
2. Cancel less than 48 hours before pickup: we keep one rental day. You get the rest back.
3. No-show (you do not come and do not tell us): we keep one rental day. The rest of the reservation is released.
4. Changes to dates or car type are free when a car is available. The price follows the new dates.
5. Flight delayed? Send us your flight number. We hold the car until the next morning at no cost.
6. Returning early: unused days are not refunded on weekly or monthly rates. On daily rates we refund full unused days.
7. Refunds go back to the card or method you paid with, within 5 to 10 working days.
8. If we must cancel (for example a car is damaged and no other car is free), you get a full refund.

To cancel, reply to your booking email or message us on WhatsApp with your booking reference.

PRIVACY
Yabi Demo Rentals. Demo text, not legal advice.

WHAT WE COLLECT
- Name, email, phone and booking details.
- Driving licence details and a photo of your licence, taken at pickup.
- Photos of the car and any damage, your signature, odometer and fuel readings.
- Payment status. Card numbers are handled by our payment provider, we never see or store them.

WHY
- To run your rental, check you may legally drive, protect you and us in case of damage, and send your contract and receipts.
- We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

HOW WE KEEP IT SAFE
- Licence numbers and dates of birth are stored encrypted.
- Photos and signatures live in private storage that only our staff can open.

HOW LONG
- Rental records are kept as long as tax rules require (usually 7 years).
- Licence photos, car photos and signatures are deleted after the retention period set by the rental company, unless there is an open damage or fine case.

YOUR RIGHTS
- Ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Email us and we answer within 30 days.
- Some records (invoices, open damage cases) we must keep by law. We tell you which.

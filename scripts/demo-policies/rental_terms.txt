RENTAL RULES AND TERMS
Yabi Demo Rentals, Kralendijk, Bonaire. Demo text, not legal advice.

BEFORE YOU DRIVE
1. The main driver must be 21 or older and have held a valid licence for at least 1 year.
2. Drivers under 25 pay a young driver fee of USD 10 per day.
3. Bring your physical licence and an ID or passport to pickup. A photo of a licence is not accepted.
4. Only drivers named on this agreement may drive. Extra drivers must come to the desk, show their licence and sign. Extra driver: USD 15 per rental.
5. We walk around the car with you at pickup. Any damage we find is photographed and saved with this agreement.

SECURITY DEPOSIT
6. A refundable deposit (USD 250 for cars, USD 500 for SUVs, USD 750 for pickups and vans) is taken in cash or by card at pickup.
7. You get it back at return when the car comes back on time, with the same fuel, clean, and with no new damage.
8. Card deposits are released within 72 hours. Bank timing can take a few days more.

WHERE AND HOW YOU DRIVE
9. Bonaire only. The car may not leave the island or go on any ferry or boat.
10. Paved roads and the normal roads into Washington Slagbaai Park are fine. No beach driving, no off-road tracks, no driving through salt pans or water.
11. Drive on the right. Speed limits: 40 km/h in town, 60 km/h outside town. Roundabouts: traffic on the roundabout goes first.
12. Watch for goats, donkeys and iguanas on the road. Do not feed the donkeys from the car.
13. No drinking and driving, no phone in hand while driving. Seatbelts for everyone. Children under 1.35 m use a child seat (ask for one at the desk).
14. No smoking or vaping in the car. Cleaning fee: USD 100.
15. No pets unless agreed at the desk. Sand and wet dive gear are fine, please shake off what you can.
16. Never leave valuables, dive gear or bags visible in a parked car, especially at dive sites. Lock the car every time.
17. Traffic and parking fines during your rental are yours. We pass them on with a USD 15 handling fee.

FUEL, KEYS AND RETURN
18. You get the car with a fuel level noted at pickup. Bring it back at the same level. Missing fuel: USD 3 per liter plus USD 15 service.
19. Return the car at the agreed time. We allow 59 minutes grace. After that each started day is charged at the daily rate.
20. Lost key or remote: USD 150.
21. Need more days? Message us before your return time and we extend if the car is free.

DAMAGE, ACCIDENTS AND BREAKDOWNS
22. Accident or damage: stop, stay safe, call the police (911) and call us right away. Take photos. Do not admit fault or pay anyone on the spot.
23. Without a police report the insurance does not cover the damage.
24. Basic cover is included with your own risk up to the deposit amount. Standard and Premium cover lower that risk. Tires, glass, the underside and the interior are only covered with Premium.
25. Damage from breaking these rules (off-road, beach, drink driving, an unlisted driver) is never covered. You pay the full repair.
26. Breakdown: call us. We bring a replacement car when one is available. Do not let anyone else repair the car.

PAYMENT
27. The rental is paid at booking or at pickup. Prices are in USD.
28. Damage, missing fuel, fines and late return can be taken from the deposit. We explain every amount we keep, in writing.

By signing you confirm you read these rules, the car condition at pickup is recorded correctly, and you agree to follow them.

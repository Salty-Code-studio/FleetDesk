RENTAL RULES AND TERMS
Yabi Demo Rentals, Kralendijk, Bonaire. Demo text, not legal advice.

FULL TERMS

Before you drive
1. The main driver must be 21 or older and have held a valid licence for at least 1 year.
2. Drivers under 25 pay a young driver fee of USD 10 per day.
3. Bring your physical licence and an ID or passport to pickup. A photo of a licence is not accepted.
4. Extra drivers come to the desk, show their licence and sign. Extra driver: USD 15 per rental.
5. We walk around the car with you at pickup. Any damage we find is photographed and saved with this agreement.

Security deposit
6. A refundable deposit (USD 250 for cars, USD 500 for SUVs, USD 750 for pickups and vans) is taken in cash or by card at pickup.
7. You get it back at return when the car comes back on time, full, clean and with no new damage.
8. Card deposits are released within 72 hours. Bank timing can take a few days more.

On the road
9. Paved roads and the normal roads into Washington Slagbaai Park are fine.
10. Drive on the right. Speed limits: 40 km/h in town, 60 km/h outside town. On a roundabout, traffic already on it goes first.
11. Watch for goats, donkeys and iguanas on the road. Do not feed the donkeys from the car.
12. Children under 1.35 m ride in a child seat. Ask for one at the desk.
13. No pets unless agreed at the desk. Shake sand and wet dive gear off before you get in.

Keys, fuel and return
14. Lost key or remote: USD 150.
15. Need more days? Message us before your return time and we extend if the car is free.

Damage, accidents and breakdowns
16. Do not admit fault or pay anyone on the spot. Take photos.
17. Basic cover is included, with your own risk up to the deposit amount. Standard and Premium cover lower that risk. Tires, glass, the underside and the interior are only covered with Premium.
18. Damage from breaking these rules (off-road, beach, drink driving, an unlisted driver, keys left in the car) is never covered. You pay the full repair.
19. Breakdown: call us. We bring a replacement car when one is free. Do not let anyone else repair the car.

Payment
20. The rental is paid at booking or at pickup. Prices are in USD.
21. Damage, missing fuel, cleaning, fines and late return can be taken from the deposit. We explain every amount we keep, in writing.

By signing you confirm you read and ticked every key rule, the car condition at pickup is recorded correctly, and you agree to follow these terms.
